Viet74K Vietnamese word and phrase list, compiled by Hồ Ngọc Đức.
Original project: http://www.informatik.uni-leipzig.de/~duc/software/misc/wordlist.html
Source snapshot: https://github.com/duyet/vietnamese-wordlist/blob/688b0953c8cdb21ee090ce80b83cefdb052fe2cd/Viet74K.txt

This version normalizes Unicode to NFC, normalizes spacing, keeps Latin-alphabet
entries with spaces or hyphens, removes case-insensitive duplicates, and splits
the resulting 71810 words and phrases into separate UTF-8 files.
