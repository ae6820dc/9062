Input guide

Use a decimal point, scientific notation such as 1e-6, and a minus sign for negatives. Matrices use spaces or commas between entries and newlines or semicolons between rows.

Functions support explicit multiplication (2*x), powers (x^2), constants pi and e, and sin cos tan asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh exp log log10 sqrt abs cbrt sign. All angles are in radians. log is the natural logarithm. Real-valued finite evaluations are required.

Accuracy & your data

Numerical modes use IEEE 754 double precision (about 15–16 significant digits). Display precision changes formatting, not computational accuracy. Number theory and combinatorics use exact integers. Ill-conditioned problems may lose accuracy; inspect residuals and convergence notes.

All calculation runs in your browser, with a cancel button and a 15-second work limit. No data is sent to a server. Local storage is separate for every calculator. Keep an exported backup before clearing browser data. Plots are sampled visual aids and can miss discontinuities.
