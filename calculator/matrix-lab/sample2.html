Input guide

Use a decimal point, scientific notation such as 1e-6, and a minus sign for negatives. Matrices use spaces or commas between entries and newlines or semicolons between rows.

Functions support explicit multiplication (2*x), powers (x^2), constants pi and e, and sin cos tan asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh exp log log10 sqrt abs cbrt sign. All angles are in radians. log is the natural logarithm. Real-valued finite evaluations are required.

Accuracy & your data

New high-precision modes use math.js BigNumber decimal algorithms at 16–1000 significant digits plus 20 guard digits. Exact rational modes use Fraction BigInt arithmetic. Original modes retain double precision (about 15–16 digits), except exact integer operations. A precision setting is a working target, not a guarantee of correct digits. Symbolic transformations may alter domains. Ill-conditioned problems may lose accuracy; inspect residuals and convergence notes.

All calculation runs in your browser, with a cancel button and a configurable time limit. No data is sent to a server. Local storage is separate for every calculator. Keep an exported backup before clearing browser data. Plots are sampled visual aids and can miss discontinuities.
